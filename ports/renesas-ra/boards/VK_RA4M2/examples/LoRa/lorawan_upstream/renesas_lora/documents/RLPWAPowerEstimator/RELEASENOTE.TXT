[ Limitation ]
  - Supply voltage is currently supported only 3.0[V].
  - Operating temperature is currently supported only at 25 degrees Celsius.
  - It is conceivable that the modulation method, RF parameters (SF, CR etc.) are
    influenced, but it is not taken into consideration at present.
  - In actual devices, the current during transmission increases with
    the decrease of the power supply voltage, but it is not considered at present.
